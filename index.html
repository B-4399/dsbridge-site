PCC Legalization.

Here is Akash an Indian national who was residing in the UAE. He got an enrollment into a Lithuanian University and followed every procedures, submitted his digital copies for TRP evaluation, got an appointment date at the UAE external service provider. His PCC needed an extra stamp from the UAE consulate in Riga-Latvia as stated by MOFA Lithuania and he knew of no one in Europe who could take his PCC to Latvia for an extra stamp. With just 4 days to his appointment date, Akash got anxious but was later helped by a Nepali lady who happened to have had a similar problem but had a friend in Europe who could help with her PCC.Akash got his PCC from Lithuania in the morning on the day of his appointment date.
